Daily tarot readings, task management, and journaling — all in one app. Bring a touch of mystery to your everyday life.

[One Oracle Tarot Reading]
Draw one card from 22 Major Arcana for a simple yet powerful tarot reading. Receive today's message with a beautiful flip animation. Supports both upright and reversed positions.

[TODO Management]
Add tasks with due dates. Filter by complete or incomplete. Plus, a unique feature lets you divine the fortune of each individual task with tarot.

[Diary]
Record your daily moments. Attach photos. Protect your precious entries with a password lock that activates automatically when the app goes to background.

[Calendar]
Select dates on a monthly calendar. View that day's tarot reading and tasks at a glance.

[Privacy]
All data is stored only on your device. Nothing is ever sent to external servers. Your data belongs to you alone.

[Languages]
